How to Use
A quick guide to everything in your Wedding Guest List & Meal Planner.
Before you start: This app saves your data to your browser automatically. For safety, download a backup regularly using the Download Backup button in Settings. If you clear your browser data or switch browsers, your data will not transfer automatically.
1
Set up your wedding details
Head to the Settings tab first. Enter both partner names, your wedding date, and pick a theme color. Your names will appear in the header throughout the app.
2
Add your guests
On the Guest List tab, tap + Add Guest to open the form. Choose Single Guest for an individual, or Group / Household for a family or group sharing one entry. Fill in their name, side, RSVP status, meal choice, and any events they are attending.
3
Track and update RSVPs
Each guest card shows a colored RSVP badge. Tap it to cycle through Not Invited, Invited, Confirmed, and Declined. To edit more details, tap anywhere on the card to expand it and update fields directly.
4
Adding a plus-one
When adding or editing a single guest, check Has Plus-One. A new section will appear where you can enter the plus-one's name and meal preference.
5
Groups and households
Select Group / Household for families or groups. Set the party size and the form shows a row for each seat where you can enter a name, meal, and highchair need. Names are optional but helpful for meal planning. The whole household shares one RSVP status and one address.
6
Managing highchair needs
For groups and households, each seat has a Highchair checkbox. Tick it for any member that needs one when adding or editing the guest. The total is counted automatically. On the Guest List tab, the Highchairs stat card is clickable to see a summary of everyone who needs one.
7
Tracking events
Go to the Events tab to see how many guests are attending each event. You can add, rename, or remove event categories at the bottom of the Events page. Click any event card to open a popup where you can toggle attendance for every guest. For households and guests with plus-ones, tap the guest to expand a list of individual members and select exactly who from the group is coming to that event.
8
Managing meals
The Meals tab shows how many guests chose each meal option. Click any meal card to see a list of exactly who selected it, and update meals right from that popup without opening each guest profile. You can add, rename, or remove meal categories at the bottom of the Meals page.
9
Exporting your data
In Settings, tap Export CSV or Export PDF to download your full guest list.
10
Back up your data regularly
In Settings, use Download Backup to save a copy of your data as a file. To restore, tap Import Backup and select that file. Backups can also be shared across different devices. Your data is stored locally in your browser, so a regular backup is the best way to keep it safe.
Important Notes
Please read these before you start using your tracker.
1
Clearing Browser History or Cache Deletes Your Data
Don't clear your browser's cache, cookies, or history. If you do, all your data will be deleted.
2
Download Backups Regularly
After finishing each section, go to Settings and tap Download Backup. Save the file to your Desktop or Google Drive. Use Import Backup to restore your data.
3
Data Lives Locally on Your Device
All your data is stored only in your browser on your device.
4
Always Download a Backup Before Clearing History
If you need to clear your browser history, download a backup file first so you can restore your data.
5
Never Use Private or Incognito Mode
Never open this file in a private or incognito window. Closing that window permanently deletes all your data.
6
Don't Save Tracker Files in Cloud Storage
Don't save the files in Google Drive, iCloud, or other cloud storage. Data is stored in your browser, not in cloud files.
7
Reinstalling Your Browser Erases Everything
If you reinstall your browser or reset it to factory settings, all your data will be deleted.
8
Don't Switch Browsers
Chrome, Safari, and Firefox each save data separately. Use the same browser every time, or import a backup to switch.
9
Chrome Profiles Have Separate Storage
If you use multiple profiles in Chrome, each profile has its own data. Import a backup to move data between profiles.
10
Cleanup Apps Will Erase Your Data
Apps like CleanMyMac and CCleaner delete everything, including your data. This is the most common reason people lose their data.
11
Turn Off Browser Auto-Clear Settings
Some browsers automatically delete all site data when you close the browser. Turn this off in your browser's privacy settings.
12
Data Doesn't Sync Between Devices
Your data on your iPhone won't appear on your Mac automatically. To move data between devices, download a backup on one device and import it on the other.
13
Be Cautious on Shared Computers
If you use a shared computer, be careful. Someone else might clear the browser data. Keep a backup copy saved somewhere only you can access.
